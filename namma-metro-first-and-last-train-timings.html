The Majestic 11:25 PM synchronized last train

To ensure commuters interchanging between Purple and Green Lines do not get stranded at night, BMRCL holds connecting trains at Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Station (Majestic) until 11:25 PM. If you arrive at Majestic on an 11:00 PM departure from Challaghatta or Silk Institute, your connecting train towards Whitefield or Madavara will wait at the platform until all transfer passengers cross over.

Peak vs non-peak train frequencies

  • Morning Peak (8:30 AM – 10:30 AM): A train arrives every 3 to 5 minutes on the Purple Line central stretch (between Vijayanagar and Baiyappanahalli), and every 5 to 6 minutes on the Green Line.
  • Midday Off-Peak (11:00 AM – 4:30 PM): A train arrives every 8 to 10 minutes across all active lines.
  • Evening Peak (5:30 PM – 8:30 PM): A train arrives every 4 to 5 minutes on the Purple Line, and every 5 to 7 minutes on the Green Line.
  • Late Night (after 9:30 PM): Frequency widens to 12 to 15 minutes between trains.

Frequently asked questions

What time does the first Namma Metro train start in the morning?

On Monday through Saturday, the first train departs terminal stations (Challaghatta, Whitefield, Madavara, Silk Institute) at 5:00 AM. On Sundays, services start at 7:00 AM.

What time does the last Namma Metro train leave at night?

The last trains depart from terminal stations at 11:00 PM on all days of the week. Connecting trains are coordinated at Majestic interchange until 11:25 PM.

Does Namma Metro run late during IPL matches or New Year celebrations?

Yes. BMRCL regularly extends train operations until 1:00 AM or 2:00 AM on New Year's Eve and during IPL cricket matches played at M. Chinnaswamy Stadium (Cubbon Park and MG Road stations).
