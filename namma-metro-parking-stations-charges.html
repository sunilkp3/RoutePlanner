Stations with official parking lots

Purple Line parking stations

  • Whitefield (Kadugodi): Large surface lot for two-wheelers and cars; ideal for commuters from Hoskote and eastern outskirts.
  • Kadugodi Tree Park & Pattandur Agrahara: Ample two-wheeler parking for Whitefield tech workers.
  • Swami Vivekananda Road (Old Madras Road): Dedicated multi-level automated car and two-wheeler parking facility.
  • Baiyappanahalli: Massive ground parking area for both cars and bikes near the Sir M. Visvesvaraya Railway Terminal.
  • Deepanjali Nagar & Mysuru Road: High-capacity two-wheeler and car parking lots serving western commuters.
  • Jnanabharathi & Kengeri: Dedicated parking facilities for Bengaluru University students and Mysuru Road commuters.
  • Challaghatta: Surface parking at the western terminal.

Green Line parking stations

  • Nagasandra: Major transit hub parking serving Tumkur Road, Nelamangala, and northern commuters.
  • Dasarahalli & Jalahalli: Two-wheeler parking facilities.
  • Peenya Industry & Goraguntepalya: Two-wheeler lots for industrial estate and Ring Road commuters.
  • Yeshwanthpur: High-capacity multi-modal parking facility connected to the railway station.
  • Banashankari: Integrated parking opposite the BMTC TTMC bus terminal.
  • Yelachenahalli, Konanakunte Cross & Silk Institute: Generous car and two-wheeler parking serving Kanakapura Road communities.

Electric vehicle (EV) charging facilities

BMRCL has installed public EV charging stations at Baiyappanahalli, Swami Vivekananda Road, Mysuru Road, and Nagasandra stations in partnership with BESCOM. Riders can charge two-wheelers and four-wheelers during their work day using the BESCOM EV Mithra mobile app.

Frequently asked questions

Which Namma Metro stations have car and bike parking?

Major terminal and suburban stations have dedicated multi-level or surface parking lots, including Whitefield (Kadugodi), Swami Vivekananda Road, Baiyappanahalli, Mysuru Road, Jnanabharathi, Kengeri, Nagasandra, Yeshwanthpur, and Silk Institute.

What are the parking charges at Namma Metro stations?

Standard two-wheeler parking is ₹15 for the first 4 hours and ₹5 for every additional hour (maximum ₹30 per day). Four-wheeler car parking is ₹30 for the first 4 hours and ₹10 per additional hour (maximum ₹60 per day).

Is overnight parking permitted at Namma Metro stations?

Overnight parking is permitted at select 24-hour lots (such as Baiyappanahalli, Swami Vivekananda Road, and Nagasandra) with overnight surcharge slips, but unattended vehicles left for more than 48 hours without prior notice may be towed for security reasons.
