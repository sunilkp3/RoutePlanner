Start from the correct Kengeri station

Kengeri, Kengeri Bus Terminal and Challaghatta are distinct stops in the stored network. This guide begins at Kengeri. If your bus or pickup drops you at another station, change the origin in the planner. The difference affects the stop count and the directions to the station entrance, even though the train direction towards the city remains the same.

Passing Majestic without changing

Stay on the Purple Line through Majestic. Interchange symbols on a map mean that another line is available; they do not require you to leave the train. After Majestic, the route passes Central College, Vidhana Soudha and Cubbon Park before MG Road. Use the station sequence to recognise the approach to your destination.

Meeting someone near MG Road

Confirm the meeting point and side of the road before exiting. The metro station is one arrival point within the city centre, and the final walk may include crossings or a detour to a building entrance. For the return, take the Purple Line towards Challaghatta and leave at Kengeri, rather than riding all the way to the terminus.
