Indiranagar to Majestic by metro

This is a direct Purple Line trip west. At Indiranagar, board the platform signed towards Challaghatta and leave at Majestic (Nadaprabhu Kempegowda station).

Step by step

  1. Board at Indiranagar: take the Purple Line towards Challaghatta from Platform 2. Ride 7 stops to Nadaprabhu Kempegowda (Majestic).
  2. Exit at Nadaprabhu Kempegowda (Majestic): tap your card, scan your QR ticket or drop your token at the exit gate.

Good to know

At Majestic you can change to the Green Line for Yeshwanthpur, Rajajinagar, Chickpet or the south of the city. You can also walk to KSR Bengaluru City railway station and the Kempegowda bus station.

On the way the train stops at MG Road, Cubbon Park and Vidhana Soudha. That makes this the quickest way from Indiranagar into the central business district.

When trains run

On Tuesday to Friday, Purple Line trains towards Challaghatta run from about 5:00 AM until about 10:45 PM from the end of the line. At the busiest times a train comes every 8 minutes, and early or late in the day the gap grows to 20 minutes. Monday and weekend times are on the Purple Line page.

How the fare is worked out

BMRCL charges by distance. This trip is roughly 7.6 km measured station to station in straight lines, which falls in the ₹40 slab. The track is a little longer than straight lines, so a trip close to a slab boundary may cost one slab more. See the fares and tickets guide for every slab and the cheaper ways to pay.

Frequently asked questions

How many stops from Indiranagar to Majestic by metro?

There are 7 stops from Indiranagar to Majestic with no change of train.

What is the metro fare from Indiranagar to Majestic?

The fare is about ₹40 as a token, based on BMRCL's distance slabs. Smart card and QR ticket fares are usually a little lower. Check the fare shown at the ticket counter or in the official app before you travel.

How long does the Indiranagar to Majestic metro take?

Allow about 18 minutes on the train, plus time to get through security and to the platform.
