Indiranagar to Majestic Metro Route

A direct Purple Line journey into the central interchange. Majestic is your destination on this route; passing through a station that serves two lines does not itself mean changing trains.

7 stops after boarding · 8 stations including endpoints · 0 changes

Journey instructions

  1. Purple Line

    Board at Indiranagar

    Take the train towards Challaghatta. Ride 7 stops to Nadaprabhu Kempegowda (Majestic). Leave the train here.

Which train to board

At Indiranagar, use the Purple Line direction towards Challaghatta. Majestic is an intermediate stop before that terminus. A train destination display does not need to say “Majestic” for this journey. Check that the train serves your stop, then stay aboard through Halasuru, Trinity, MG Road, Cubbon Park, Vidhana Soudha and Central College.

Connecting with a bus or railway service

“Majestic” can refer to the metro interchange or the surrounding transport district. A metro arrival is not an arrival at your bus bay or railway platform. Confirm the exact operator, terminal, station entrance and reporting time for the onward service. If your destination is KSR Bengaluru railway station, compare the separate Krantivira Sangolli Rayanna Railway Station metro stop and its access with exiting at Majestic; choose for the entrance you actually need.

Returning to Indiranagar

On the return trip, use the Purple Line towards Whitefield (Kadugodi), then leave at Indiranagar. Purple Line signs matter at Majestic because the Green Line shares the interchange. Check the direction on the station display each time; an outward platform number cannot safely be reused for the return.
