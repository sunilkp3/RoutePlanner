Majestic to Whitefield by metro

This is a single-train trip on the Purple Line. Board at Majestic on the Purple Line level, on the platform signed towards Whitefield (Kadugodi), and stay on until the last stop.

Step by step

  1. Board at Nadaprabhu Kempegowda (Majestic): take the Purple Line towards Whitefield (Kadugodi) from Platform 1 (Level 1). Ride 22 stops to Whitefield (Kadugodi).
  2. Exit at Whitefield (Kadugodi): tap your card, scan your QR ticket or drop your token at the exit gate.

Good to know

Many people take this trip from KSR Bengaluru City railway station or the Kempegowda bus station to the Whitefield tech parks. On the way the train stops at MG Road, Indiranagar, K. R. Pura and the ITPL area. Leave at Pattandur Agrahara or Kadugodi Tree Park if your office is closer to those, rather than riding to the terminal.

Weekday mornings towards Whitefield are among the most crowded trips on the network. If you travel before 8 AM you are much more likely to get a seat.

When trains run

On Tuesday to Friday, Purple Line trains towards Whitefield (Kadugodi) run from about 5:00 AM until about 11:05 PM from the end of the line. At the busiest times a train comes every 8 minutes, and early or late in the day the gap grows to 20 minutes. Monday and weekend times are on the Purple Line page.

How the fare is worked out

BMRCL charges by distance. This trip is roughly 23.8 km measured station to station in straight lines, which falls in the ₹80 slab. The track is a little longer than straight lines, so a trip close to a slab boundary may cost one slab more. See the fares and tickets guide for every slab and the cheaper ways to pay.

Frequently asked questions

How many stops from Majestic to Whitefield by metro?

There are 22 stops from Majestic to Whitefield with no change of train.

What is the metro fare from Majestic to Whitefield?

The fare is about ₹80 as a token, based on BMRCL's distance slabs. Smart card and QR ticket fares are usually a little lower. Check the fare shown at the ticket counter or in the official app before you travel.

How long does the Majestic to Whitefield metro take?

Allow about 55 minutes on the train, plus time to get through security and to the platform.
