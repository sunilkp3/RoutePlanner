Namma Metro Lost and Found: Retrieval Steps & Helplines

Every year thousands of wallets, laptops, umbrellas, and mobile phones are left on Bengaluru Metro seats. BMRCL operates a systematic Lost and Found cataloging procedure across all stations.

Step-by-step retrieval procedure

  1. Alert the nearest Station Controller: Walk to the Station Control Room (located near the customer care desk) at the station where you exited. Provide the train direction, approximate coach number (front, middle, or rear), and time of travel.
  2. Station Controller coordinates with train staff: If the train is still in transit or at the terminal loop (Whitefield, Challaghatta, Madavara, or Silk Institute), the station staff will radio train security to inspect the seat.
  3. Item logged in Station Register: If recovered, the item is logged with a unique tracking number in the station's official Lost and Found register.
  4. Claiming your property: Present a government photo identity card (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID) and verifiable proof of ownership (unlocking the smartphone, displaying matching serial numbers, or describing distinctive internal contents).

Central Lost Property Office at Yeshwanthpur

If an item is not claimed at the local station within 48 hours, it is transferred under secure custody to the BMRCL Central Lost Property Office (LPO) located at Yeshwanthpur Metro Station (Green Line).

  • Location: Concourse Level, Yeshwanthpur Metro Station, Bengaluru - 560022.
  • Operating Hours: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 5:30 PM (closed on Sundays and public holidays).
  • Storage duration: Unclaimed general items are retained for up to 6 months before public auction or donation according to BMRCL regulations.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I forget an item inside a Namma Metro train?

Immediately report it to the Station Controller at your destination station. Station controllers coordinate with the train operator and the terminal depot security to secure your item.

Where is the Central Lost Property Office for Bengaluru Metro?

Items unclaimed within 48 hours at local stations are deposited at the BMRCL Central Lost Property Office located at Yeshwanthpur Metro Station (Concourse level, Green Line).

What is the official Namma Metro customer care helpline number?

You can reach BMRCL customer care by calling toll-free 1800-425-12345 or landline 080-25191091, or by emailing travelhelp@bmrc.co.in.
