Jayanagar to Electronic City by metro

This trip needs one change. Take the Green Line one stop south to Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road (RV Road), then change to the Yellow Line towards Delta Electronics Bommasandra.

Step by step

  1. Board at Jayanagar: take the Green Line towards Silk Institute from Platform 2. Ride 1 stop to Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road.
  2. Change at Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road: take the Yellow Line towards Delta electronics Bommasandra from Platform 3 (Yellow Line Concourse). Ride 11 stops to Electronic City.
  3. Exit at Electronic City: tap your card, scan your QR ticket or drop your token at the exit gate.

Good to know

The Yellow Line passes BTM Layout and Central Silk Board, so you skip the Silk Board junction that makes this trip slow by road. For the Infosys campus, stay on one more stop to Infosys Foundation Konappana Agrahara.

Yellow Line trains run less often than Green Line trains. If you arrive at RV Road just after a Yellow Line train has left, expect a longer wait than usual, especially early in the morning.

When trains run

On Tuesday to Friday, Green Line trains towards Silk Institute run from about 5:00 AM until about 10:57 PM from the end of the line. At the busiest times a train comes every 7 minutes, and early or late in the day the gap grows to 15 minutes. Monday and weekend times are on the Green Line page.

How the fare is worked out

BMRCL charges by distance. This trip is roughly 13.5 km measured station to station in straight lines, which falls in the ₹60 slab. The track is a little longer than straight lines, so a trip close to a slab boundary may cost one slab more. See the fares and tickets guide for every slab and the cheaper ways to pay.

There are 12 stops from Jayanagar to Electronic City, with 1 change of train.

What is the metro fare from Jayanagar to Electronic City?

The fare is about ₹60 as a token, based on BMRCL's distance slabs. Smart card and QR ticket fares are usually a little lower. Check the fare shown at the ticket counter or in the official app before you travel.

How long does the Jayanagar to Electronic City metro take?

Allow about 35 minutes on the train, including the change, plus time to get through security and to the platform.
