Jayanagar to Electronic City Metro Route

This is a two-line journey: one stop on the Green Line to RV Road, followed by the Yellow Line towards Bommasandra. The transfer is required; there is no single through train in this stored route.

Journey instructions

  1. Green Line

    Board at Jayanagar

    Take the train towards Silk Institute. Ride 1 stop to Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road. Leave the train and follow signs for the next line.

  2. Yellow Line

    Change at Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road

    Take the train towards Delta electronics Bommasandra. Ride 11 stops to Electronic City. Leave the train here.

Make the change at RV Road

From Jayanagar, board in the Green Line direction towards Silk Institute and leave at the next station, Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road. Follow Yellow Line interchange signs and board towards Delta electronics Bommasandra. Do not remain on the Green Line: its next stop is Banashankari, which is not part of this route. The Yellow Line leg passes Ragigudda, Jayadeva Hospital and Central Silk Board before continuing towards Electronic City.

Electronic City station and your final destination

This route ends at the station named Electronic City. Nearby destinations may instead be better served by Infosys Foundation Konappana Agrahara or another Yellow Line stop. Ask your host for the building entrance and compare the last-mile route before buying a ticket. A station name alone does not verify an accessible walking path into a private campus or across a divided road.

Plan the return transfer separately

Return on the Yellow Line towards Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road. At RV Road, change to the Green Line towards Madavara and ride one stop to Jayanagar. The return train directions differ on both legs. Near the end of service, confirm that the connecting Green Line service will still be available when you arrive.
