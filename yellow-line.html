Namma Metro Yellow Line: stations, timings and tips

The Yellow Line runs south-east from Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road (RV Road), past Ragigudda, Jayadeva Hospital, BTM Layout and Central Silk Board, down Hosur Road through Bommanahalli, Electronic City and the Hebbagodi industrial belt to Delta Electronics Bommasandra.

Who uses the Yellow Line

It is the metro option for the Electronic City and Hosur Road tech corridor. Before it opened, that trip meant crawling through the Silk Board junction by road. Office commuters from Jayanagar, BTM and the Green Line change trains at RV Road.

Tips for riding the Yellow Line

  • Trains run less often than on the Purple and Green Lines, especially early in the morning and late at night. Check the frequency table before you leave.
  • For the Infosys campus, Infosys Foundation Konappana Agrahara station is usually closer than Electronic City station.
  • Central Silk Board station lets you skip the junction on foot. Use the marked exits for the Outer Ring Road side.

Frequently asked questions

How many stations are on the Namma Metro Yellow Line?

This guide lists 16 stations on the Yellow Line, from Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road to Delta electronics Bommasandra.

What time does the first Yellow Line train start?

On Tuesday to Friday, the first Yellow Line train leaves the end of the line at about 6:00 AM. Monday and weekend start times are different. The timings table above lists every day.

Where can I change from the Yellow Line to other lines?

The Yellow Line meets the Green Line at Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road (RV Road).
