About Namma Metro Navigator

Namma Metro Navigator, published at blrmetro.com, is an independent station-to-station planning website for Bengaluru. Its purpose is to make the sequence of stops, train directions and line changes easier to understand, especially when a journey uses more than one line.

What the site provides

The planner covers the Purple, Green and Yellow lines in its stored dataset. It lists every station on a selected journey, separates the journey into legs and lets you reverse the route for the trip home. Six public route guides add practical decisions such as choosing an Electronic City destination station, passing through Majestic without changing and allowing for onward travel.

Publisher and accountability

The site is maintained under the Namma Metro Navigator name. Its website source and change history are public in the sunilkp3/RoutePlanner repository. Site feedback and corrections can be sent through the published @blrmetro account. We do not claim operator affiliation or access to internal BMRCL systems.

Editorial approach

Route instructions are derived from the stored station graph; guidance explains how to use those instructions in a real journey. The site avoids publishing numerical fares, platform assignments or next-train times without the required data. The methodology page documents these limits and the references to check before travel.

For operator assistance

Ticketing, service disruptions, accessibility support and lost property require BMRCL assistance. Use the official BMRCL website. This site cannot issue tickets, process refunds or confirm that a particular train is running.

Independent Bengaluru Metro journey guidance

BLRMetro is not affiliated with BMRCL. Routes cover the Purple, Green and Yellow lines stored in this site. Confirm current operations, ticket prices and boarding information with BMRCL and station displays.
