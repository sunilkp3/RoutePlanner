Understand your connection

At Majestic

Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Station, Majestic connects the Purple and Green lines in this planner. Change only if your journey instructions name a different line. Kengeri to MG Road, for example, stays on the Purple Line through Majestic.

At RV Road

Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road connects the Green and Yellow lines. Jayanagar to Electronic City needs a change here. Silk Institute to Majestic remains on the Green Line; an interchange symbol does not mean every passenger changes.

Read the terminus

The train direction names the end of its line, while your destination can be an intermediate stop. For Indiranagar to Majestic, look for the Purple Line direction towards Challaghatta, then leave at Majestic.

Make a door-to-door plan

  1. Choose your real boarding station. Area names are not always station names. Kengeri and Kengeri Bus Terminal, for example, are separate stops. Search the station you will enter.
  2. Check the last mile. Whitefield and Electronic City cover large areas. Locate your building entrance before deciding which station to use. Nearby campuses and divided roads can make a straight-line distance misleading.
  3. Check service and tickets. This planner gives a route through its stored network, not a guarantee that trains are running now. Check BMRCL for current operating information and the ticket price for your exact station pair.
  4. Allow for the parts outside the train. Include station entry, waiting, walking at interchanges and the final walk. Confirm connections with buses or intercity trains separately.

What you can verify before leaving

Route directions and line changes come from station order. Check the operator's sources for fares, service hours and boarding information, which depend on the day, time and ticket type.

  • BMRCL metro network — check the operator's network and route information.
  • BMRCL metro timings — check service hours and published operating changes.
  • BMRCL fare and ticket rules — use the official ticketing channels for your station pair.
