Bengaluru Namma Metro guides

Use this Namma Metro route finder to plan Bengaluru Metro trips across the Purple, Green and Yellow Lines. Search a place, address or metro station to see the stops, where to change lines, the estimated fare, which platform to use and when the next trains leave. With location turned on, it also shows the nearest station.

  • Yellow Line: Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road to Delta Electronics Bommasandra, via BTM Layout, Central Silk Board and Electronic City.
