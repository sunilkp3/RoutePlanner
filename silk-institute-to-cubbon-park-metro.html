Silk Institute to Cubbon Park Metro Route

Travel north on the Green Line, change at Majestic and continue east on the Purple Line. Cubbon Park is the third stop after the interchange on this route.

18 stops after boarding · 19 stations including endpoints · 1 changes

Journey instructions

  1. Green Line

    Board at Silk Institute

    Take the train towards Madavara. Ride 15 stops to Nadaprabhu Kempegowda (Majestic). Leave the train and follow signs for the next line.

  2. Purple Line

    Change at Nadaprabhu Kempegowda (Majestic)

    Take the train towards Whitefield (Kadugodi). Ride 3 stops to Cubbon Park. Leave the train here.

Use Majestic for the line change

Board the Green Line towards Madavara at Silk Institute. Remain on the Green Line through RV Road; that station offers a Yellow Line connection, but it is not needed here. At Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Station, Majestic, leave the Green Line train and follow Purple Line signs towards Whitefield (Kadugodi).

Recognise the final three stops

After changing at Majestic, the Purple Line stops at Sir M. Visvesvaraya Station, Central College; Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Station, Vidhana Soudha; then Cubbon Park. Leave at Cubbon Park. A destination elsewhere along the park boundary may require a different final walk, so check the particular gate or meeting point before choosing your exit.

Return directions and connecting service

For the return, board the Purple Line towards Challaghatta and change at Majestic to the Green Line towards Silk Institute. This is a two-train journey in each direction. Check the operator’s service information for both lines when travelling early or late; the existence of a route in this planner does not confirm that a connection is operating at the time you travel.
