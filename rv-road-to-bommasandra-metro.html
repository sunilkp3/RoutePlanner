RV Road to Bommasandra by metro

This is the full length of the Yellow Line. You board at the start of the line, so every train runs all the way to Bommasandra and you will usually get a seat.

Step by step

  1. Board at Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road: take the Yellow Line towards Delta electronics Bommasandra from Platform 3 (Yellow Line Concourse). Ride 15 stops to Delta electronics Bommasandra.
  2. Exit at Delta electronics Bommasandra: tap your card, scan your QR ticket or drop your token at the exit gate.

Good to know

If you are coming from the Green Line (Jayanagar, Banashankari or Majestic), you change at RV Road without leaving the paid area. Follow the yellow signs to the Yellow Line platform.

The Hosur Road stations, including Electronic City, Hebbagodi and Bommasandra, serve the large industrial and office campuses. Many of them run shuttles from the station. Check with your employer.

When trains run

On Tuesday to Friday, Yellow Line trains towards Delta electronics Bommasandra run from about 6:00 AM until about 11:55 PM from the end of the line. At the busiest times a train comes every 7 minutes, and early or late in the day the gap grows to 25 minutes. Monday and weekend times are on the Yellow Line page.

How the fare is worked out

BMRCL charges by distance. This trip is roughly 17.6 km measured station to station in straight lines, which falls in the ₹70 slab. The track is a little longer than straight lines, so a trip close to a slab boundary may cost one slab more. See the fares and tickets guide for every slab and the cheaper ways to pay.

Frequently asked questions

How many stops from RV Road to Bommasandra by metro?

There are 15 stops from RV Road to Bommasandra with no change of train.

What is the metro fare from RV Road to Bommasandra?

The fare is about ₹70 as a token, based on BMRCL's distance slabs. Smart card and QR ticket fares are usually a little lower. Check the fare shown at the ticket counter or in the official app before you travel.

How long does the RV Road to Bommasandra metro take?

Allow about 38 minutes on the train, plus time to get through security and to the platform.
