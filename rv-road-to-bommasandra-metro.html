RV Road to Bommasandra Metro Route

A direct ride along the represented Yellow Line from Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road to Delta electronics Bommasandra. RV Road is also a Green Line station, so choose the line as well as the direction.

Find the Yellow Line boarding area

At RV Road, follow signs for the Yellow Line towards Delta electronics Bommasandra. A Green Line train at the same interchange cannot complete this direct journey. If you are arriving on the Green Line, allow for leaving that train and walking to the Yellow Line before counting the journey in this guide. Confirm the platform at the station; this page does not assign a platform number.

Use the intermediate stops to choose your destination

The route serves Jayadeva Hospital, BTM Layout, Central Silk Board and the Electronic City corridor before Bommasandra. If you are visiting an office or industrial site, compare its entrance with Electronic City, Infosys Foundation Konappana Agrahara, Huskur Road, Biocon Hebbagodi and Bommasandra. The end station is not necessarily the nearest access point to every address in the broader area.

For a journey beyond RV Road on the way back

A return Yellow Line train runs in the direction of Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road. If your final destination is on the Green or Purple Line, entering RV Road as the destination omits that onward leg. Enter the real final metro station in the planner to obtain the additional interchange instructions and stops.
