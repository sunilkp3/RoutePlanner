How to book tickets on WhatsApp (+91 81055 56677)

  1. Start the chat: Send "Hi" to +91 81055 56677 on WhatsApp.
  2. Choose your language: Select English or Kannada.
  3. Pick stations: Tap "Buy Ticket", choose your origin station and destination station from the dropdown.
  4. Select passenger count: You can purchase single or return tickets for up to 6 passengers at once.
  5. Pay with UPI: Pay securely using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.
  6. Scan at the gate: Your phone screen displays a dynamic QR code. Hold it 5–10 cm above the glass reader on the right-hand side of the turnstile to open the gate.

Smart Card recharge rules & Card Top-up Terminals (CTT)

When you recharge your Varshik Smart Card online through the Namma Metro app or Amazon Pay, the balance is not written to your card immediately. You must tap your physical card on a Card Top-up Terminal (CTT) located near station customer care counters or at the entry gate within 15 days to update the electronic chip.

Card validity and refunds

Namma Metro smart cards remain valid for one year from the date of the last recharge. If your card expires with unspent money, visit any station customer care desk. Staff can extend card validity or transfer the remaining balance to a new card for a nominal processing fee.

Frequently asked questions

What discount do you get with a Namma Metro Smart Card or QR ticket?

Smart card holders and mobile QR ticket users receive a 5% discount on the standard token fare on every trip across Purple, Green, and Yellow lines.

How do you book Namma Metro tickets on WhatsApp?

Save the official BMRCL WhatsApp chatbot number +91 81055 56677. Send "Hi", choose your language, select originating and destination stations, select passenger count, and complete payment via UPI. A QR code is generated instantly.

What is the minimum balance required on a Namma Metro smart card?

The minimum balance required to enter a metro station is ₹50. If your card balance drops below ₹50, the entry flap gates will not open.

Can I use an NCMC card from any bank on Namma Metro?

Yes, Namma Metro automatic fare collection (AFC) gates support all RuPay National Common Mobility Cards (NCMC) issued by Indian banks including SBI, ICICI, Canara Bank, and Paytm Payments Bank.
