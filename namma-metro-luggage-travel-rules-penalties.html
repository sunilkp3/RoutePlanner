Namma Metro Luggage Limits, Prohibited Items & Rules

Every Bengaluru Metro station operates security baggage scanners and metal detectors. Understanding baggage limits, restricted items, and passenger codes of conduct prevents delays at security checkpoints.

Baggage dimensions & weight guidelines

Passengers are permitted to carry personal luggage within the following specifications:

  • Maximum dimensions: 60 cm length × 45 cm breadth × 25 cm height.
  • Maximum weight: 15 kilograms per ticketed passenger.
  • Airport luggage exception: Passengers carrying standard airline check-in suitcases to or from Kempegowda Bus Station, KSR Railway Station, or airport feeder hubs are generally accommodated if baggage fits through standard X-ray scanners and does not block carriage gangways.

Code of conduct & penalty schedule

Fines are enforced under the Metro Railways (Operation and Maintenance) Act, 2002:

  • Ticketless travel or ticket misuse: ₹500 fine plus the maximum system fare (₹90).
  • Entering the women-only reserved coach: ₹500 fine and immediate ejection from the train (the first coach in direction of travel is reserved for women).
  • Spitting, chewing tobacco, or littering: ₹500 spot fine.
  • Drunken or disorderly behavior: ₹500 fine, removal from station, and transfer to police custody.
  • Alighting on metro tracks or walking on viaducts: Up to ₹1,000 fine and imprisonment up to 6 months.
  • Eating or drinking inside carriages: Prohibited; helps maintain train cleanliness and hygiene.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum luggage allowed in Namma Metro?

Each passenger may carry one bag weighing up to 15 kg with maximum dimensions of 60 cm (length) x 45 cm (breadth) x 25 cm (height). Luggage exceeding these limits may be stopped at station baggage scanners.

Is alcohol allowed on Namma Metro?

No. Carrying open or sealed alcohol bottles is strictly prohibited on Namma Metro trains and inside station premises.

Can I carry a pet or bird on the metro in Bengaluru?

Pets and domestic animals are strictly prohibited on Namma Metro. Only certified service animals accompanying visually impaired passengers are permitted with prior station master approval.

What is the fine for travelling without a ticket on Namma Metro?

Travelling without a valid token, smart card tap, or QR ticket attracts a minimum fine of ₹500 plus the maximum distance fare slab (₹90), under the Metro Railways (Operations and Maintenance) Act.
