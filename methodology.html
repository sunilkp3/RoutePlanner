How Namma Metro Navigator works

This independent planner combines a station network stored in the website with route calculations in your browser. It is intended to help you understand a journey before travelling; it does not issue tickets or receive a live train-position feed.

Stations, landmarks and location

Station-name searches use the site's station list. Landmark searches use an external geocoding service to find a place, then associate it with a nearby station. GPS can help select a starting station when you allow it. Geographic proximity does not prove that a station has the shortest accessible walking route: entrances, crossings and restricted land matter.

Routes and interchange

The route engine connects stations along the represented lines and identifies changes of line. Check the ordered stops and interchange before leaving. Platform and direction labels are planning guidance; confirm the actual boarding platform with the station display. When a new station, renamed station or service change is missing, report the two endpoints and the incorrect step through our contact page.

Time and fare estimates

The timetable model estimates departures and station arrival times. It cannot account for every service disruption, queue or missed connection. The displayed rail time should be combined with your first and last mile travel, station entry and waiting time. Fare calculations are estimates from the site's fare model, not a current BMRCL ticket quote.

Source hierarchy and corrections

BMRCL announcements, ticketing information, station signs and staff instructions take priority over this website. Consult the official BMRCL website for current services. We avoid presenting our computed arrival times as live operational observations. Route corrections should identify the page or station pair, the incorrect value and an official source when available.

Advertising and editorial content

The journey planner does not load Google advertising. Route guidance is available without an account, GPS permission or an ad interaction. Advertising ownership verification is separate from displaying advertisements. Any future placement needs a separate content and placement review.
