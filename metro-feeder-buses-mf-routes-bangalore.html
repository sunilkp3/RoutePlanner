Ticketing and payment on feeder buses

  • Cash & UPI: Conductors issue printed tickets with QR codes for UPI payments (GPay, PhonePe).
  • BMTC Daily Pass: If you hold a ₹70 standard BMTC daily bus pass, it is valid on all non-AC MF routes without paying extra.
  • Vajra AC Pass: If travelling on air-conditioned feeder buses, a ₹120 Vajra daily pass is valid.

First and last feeder bus timings

Most MF series buses begin operations at 6:00 AM from metro station bays and run until 10:30 PM, aligning with train arrival schedules at terminal and interchange stations.

Frequently asked questions

What does MF stand for in BMTC bus numbers?

MF stands for "Metro Feeder". These are dedicated non-AC and AC bus routes operated by BMTC specifically designed to connect metro stations to residential neighborhoods and tech parks that do not have direct rail access.

Can I pay for Metro Feeder buses with UPI or metro smart cards?

BMTC conductors carry electronic ticketing machines that support dynamic UPI QR code payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm). Metro smart cards are not currently integrated with BMTC buses, but BMTC daily and monthly bus passes are accepted on non-AC feeder routes.

Which metro stations have the best feeder bus connectivity to Outer Ring Road tech parks?

K.R. Pura, Swami Vivekananda Road, and Baiyappanahalli have the highest frequency of feeder buses heading towards Marathahalli, Kadubeesanahalli, Bellandur, and Sarjapur Road.
