Majestic Metro Interchange Guide: Purple and Green Lines

Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Station (Majestic) is Bengaluru's central underground transit interchange where the Purple Line and Green Line cross. Here is how to navigate between levels quickly and safely.

How to change trains step by step

  1. Step off your train: Note whether you are on the Purple Line (upper track level) or Green Line (lower track level).
  2. Follow overhead colour markers: Look up at the illuminated direction boards. Follow the Purple signs for Whitefield/Challaghatta, or the Green signs for Madavara/Silk Institute.
  3. Use the central escalators or stairs: Walk towards the centre of the platform. Take the designated escalators directly connecting the Purple and Green track levels.
  4. Do not pass through turnstiles: Stay within the yellow tiled interchange corridors. Turnstiles will deduct an exit fare if tapped.
  5. Arrive at your connecting platform: Check the platform digital screens to confirm train arrival time (usually 3 to 6 minutes at peak hours).

Exits for trains and city buses

Majestic has multiple numbered gates serving different transit hubs:

  • Exit A (KSR Railway Station): Direct covered skywalk connecting directly into Platform 10 of KSR Bengaluru City Railway Station.
  • Exit B (BMTC Bus Stand): Opens straight into Kempegowda Bus Station (Majestic Bus Terminal) for city buses, Vayu Vajra airport buses, and suburban routes.
  • Exit C (KSRTC Inter-state Bus Terminal): Leads to Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation terminals for inter-city travel to Mysuru, Hassan, Mangaluru, and Tamil Nadu.

Accessibility & senior citizen transfers

All four platform levels at Majestic are connected by high-capacity glass lifts. If travelling with heavy luggage, a wheelchair, or a stroller, locate the central elevators near the station control room rather than using crowded escalators.

Frequently asked questions

How much time is needed to change trains at Majestic Metro station?

Allow between 3 to 5 minutes to change lines at Majestic. During peak rush hours (8:30 AM to 10:30 AM and 5:30 PM to 8:00 PM), allow 6 to 8 minutes due to heavier stair and escalator foot traffic.

Do I need to tap out or buy a new ticket when interchanging at Majestic?

No. You stay inside the paid concourse area. Do not tap your card or QR ticket at any gate while changing lines. Simply follow the coloured directional signs to your connecting platform.

How do I walk from Majestic Metro to KSR Bengaluru City Railway Station?

Take Exit A (towards railway station). Follow the covered pedestrian foot overbridge (FOB) that connects the metro concourse directly with Platform 10 and the main entrance of KSR Bengaluru City railway station without touching street traffic.
