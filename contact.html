Contact and corrections

For a site question, route correction or privacy request, contact Namma Metro Navigator through the published @blrmetro Instagram account. Instagram is an external service and may require an account to send a message.

Make a route report useful

Include your starting and destination stations, the incorrect direction or connection, and the page URL. A link to an official BMRCL notice or network reference helps distinguish a permanent network correction from a temporary operating change. Do not send passwords, ticket QR codes, payment information or precise personal location.

Technical problems

If the planner is blank or will not accept a station, include the browser and device type and the station names you entered. You can still use the static route guides and line lists without location access or JavaScript. The public source repository also provides a record of site changes.

Travel assistance

BLRMetro is not BMRCL customer support. For a fare dispute, lost item, service disruption or assistance at a station, use the official BMRCL website and station staff. Site feedback cannot change train operations.

Independent Bengaluru Metro journey guidance

BLRMetro is not affiliated with BMRCL. Routes cover the Purple, Green and Yellow lines stored in this site. Confirm current operations, ticket prices and boarding information with BMRCL and station displays.
