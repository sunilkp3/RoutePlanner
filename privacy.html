Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Namma Metro Navigator (blrmetro.com) uses, why, and the choices you have.

Updated 2026-10-24 · Independent guide, not an official BMRCL page

Location

If you allow location access in your browser, the route finder uses your position to find the nearest station and follow your trip. Location is processed in your browser to do this. We don't send it to our own servers or store it. You can turn location off at any time in your browser settings and type a place instead.

Place search

When you search for a landmark, the search text is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find matching places. Please don't type personal addresses you don't want to share with that service.

Data stored in your browser

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember settings such as the destination alarm. This data stays on your device, and you can clear it with your browser's site-data settings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are used and how the site performs, such as page views, device and browser type, and approximate region. Google Analytics uses cookies. You can opt out with Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

The site also shows a few labelled promotions for our own related projects. Clicking one takes you to that site, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The site is meant for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top. For questions, see the contact page.
