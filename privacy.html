Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026.

Station choices and route links

Route calculations run in your browser. When you plan a journey, the origin and destination appear in the page URL so you can return to or share it. Anyone you share that URL with can see the selected stations. Like other website requests, a visit to a route URL may appear in hosting access logs. Avoid sharing route links if they reveal travel information you wish to keep private.

Optional location

The page requests device location only after you choose “Find a nearby origin.” Your browser asks for permission. The coordinates are used once in the page to compare station distances; this implementation does not send them to a geocoding service or analytics endpoint and does not save them in browser storage. Your browser or operating system handles the underlying location service. You can deny permission and enter a station manually.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This version does not load Google Analytics, Google advertising, third-party map tiles or a remote place-search service. It does not create application cookies or browser-storage records. The advertising account meta tag and ads.txt file identify an advertising account; they do not display advertisements. Hosting infrastructure may still process request details such as IP address, URL and browser information to deliver and protect the website.

External sites

Links to BMRCL, GitHub and Instagram open services with their own privacy policies. A message you send through Instagram is handled by that service and the account receiving it. Do not include sensitive personal information in a correction report.

Questions

Use the contact page for privacy questions. If the site's data practices change, this page should be revised before the change is released.

Independent Bengaluru Metro journey guidance

BLRMetro is not affiliated with BMRCL. Routes cover the Purple, Green and Yellow lines stored in this site. Confirm current operations, ticket prices and boarding information with BMRCL and station displays.
