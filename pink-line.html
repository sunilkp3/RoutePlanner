Namma Metro Pink Line: opening date, stations and route

Upcoming, not open yet. The first stretch runs along Bannerghatta Road from Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere, with opening expected around 15 November 2026. The date is not final.

Latest status

The elevated section has been reported as cleared by the safety commissioner, and the Chief Minister has said the Prime Minister will inaugurate it on 15 November 2026. BMRCL has not published the timetable or fares that we could confirm, so check bmrc.co.in before you plan around it.

What opens later

The rest of the line continues north to Nagawara, including the underground section through the city centre. Dates for that section have changed several times and aren't confirmed, so we don't list one here.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Namma Metro Pink Line open now?

Not yet. The Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere stretch has been reported as cleared by the safety commissioner, but passenger service has not started.

When will the Pink Line open?

The Chief Minister has said the Prime Minister will inaugurate it on 15 November 2026. That date is not final, and public service may start after the inauguration. We will update this page when BMRCL confirms it.

Which stations open first on the Pink Line?

Six elevated stations: Kalena Agrahara, Hulimavu, IIM Bangalore (IIMB), JP Nagar 4th Phase, Jayadeva Hospital and Tavarekere.

How long is the first stretch of the Pink Line?

About 7.5 km. The full line is planned to run about 21 km from Kalena Agrahara to Nagawara. The rest opens later, and that section has no confirmed date.

Where does the Pink Line connect to other metro lines?

At Jayadeva Hospital, where you can change to the Yellow Line. To reach the Green Line, take the Yellow Line to RV Road.

What will the Pink Line fare be?

BMRCL has not published Pink Line fares. If the current distance slabs apply, a ride along the whole first stretch would cost about ₹40.

Does the Pink Line go to MG Road?

Not in this first stretch. The planned route reaches MG Road in the later underground section.
