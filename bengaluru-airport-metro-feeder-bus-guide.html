Reaching Bengaluru Airport (BLR) by Metro and Vayu Vajra

While Bengaluru's direct airport metro line is being built, combining Namma Metro with BMTC's air-conditioned Vayu Vajra buses is the most reliable, traffic-proof, and cost-effective way to get to Kempegowda International Airport.

Why metro + bus beats airport cabs during peak hours

Bengaluru's central corridors—including Old Madras Road, Indiranagar 100ft Road, and Hebbal Junction—frequently experience severe road gridlock during morning and evening rush hours. Taking the Purple Line or Green Line allows you to skip 15–20 kilometers of city-center bumper-to-bumper traffic before boarding an express AC bus on the wide Ballari Road highway.

Luggage handling on Vayu Vajra buses

BMTC Vayu Vajra buses feature dedicated luggage bays inside the low-floor cabin and luggage racks beneath the coach. Bus conductors assist with large suitcases at no additional baggage charge.

Upcoming Blue Line (Phase 2B) Airport Metro update

BMRCL is actively constructing the 37-kilometer Blue Line (Phase 2B) linking Central Silk Board to Kempegowda International Airport via HSR Layout, Bellandur, Marathahalli, KR Pura, Nagawara, Hebbal, and Yelahanka. Once operational, direct trains will run between the airport and the tech corridor in under 45 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reach Bengaluru Airport directly by Namma Metro today?

Not yet directly by train. The dedicated Blue Line (Phase 2B) from Central Silk Board via KR Pura and Hebbal to KIA Terminal is under construction. However, you can take the Purple or Green Line to key transfer stations and board high-frequency BMTC Vayu Vajra airport buses.

Which is the best metro station to catch an airport bus in Bengaluru?

K.R. Pura (Purple Line) and Swami Vivekananda Road (Purple Line) are the two most popular transfer points. At K.R. Pura, Vayu Vajra KIA-8 and KIA-8C run every 15 to 20 minutes towards the airport.

How much does it cost to reach the airport via Metro + Vayu Vajra bus?

The total trip costs around ₹270 to ₹350 per passenger (approx ₹40 metro fare + ₹250 to ₹300 Vayu Vajra ticket), compared to ₹1,200 to ₹1,800 for a private airport cab.
