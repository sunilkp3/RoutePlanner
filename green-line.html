Namma Metro Green Line: stations, timings and tips

The Green Line runs north–south from Madavara, beyond Nagasandra on Tumakuru Road, through Peenya, Yeshwanthpur, Rajajinagar and Majestic, then south through Chickpet, K. R. Market, Lalbagh, Jayanagar and Banashankari to Silk Institute on Kanakapura Road.

Who uses the Green Line

It is the line for the Peenya industrial area, Yeshwanthpur railway station, the old-city markets around Chickpet and K. R. Market, and the residential south: Jayanagar, J. P. Nagar and Banashankari. At Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road (RV Road) it meets the Yellow Line to Electronic City.

Tips for riding the Green Line

  • Change at Majestic for the Purple Line, and at RV Road for the Yellow Line. You don't need to exit the paid area at either interchange.
  • Yeshwanthpur metro station is a short walk from Yeshwanthpur railway station. Allow ten minutes with luggage.
  • On weekends, Chickpet and K. R. Market get very crowded around the markets. Keep your bag in front of you.

Frequently asked questions

How many stations are on the Namma Metro Green Line?

This guide lists 32 stations on the Green Line, from Madavara to Silk Institute.

What time does the first Green Line train start?

On Tuesday to Friday, the first Green Line train leaves the end of the line at about 5:00 AM. Monday and weekend start times are different. The timings table above lists every day.

Where can I change from the Green Line to other lines?

Change at Majestic for the Purple Line and at Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road (RV Road) for the Yellow Line.
